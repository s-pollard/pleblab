# opnsense router

Target production firewall/router for the homelab network.

## hardware

- Intel Core i5-8500
- 8 GB DDR4
- 128 GB SSD
- dual-port Intel 82599ES/X520-family 10 GbE SFP+ adapter
- dual-port Intel I350-family 1 GbE adapter
- Intel I219-LM 1 GbE onboard interface

## platform

OPNsense

## role

Dedicated routing and firewall appliance intended to replace the current UDM-Pro routing role.
