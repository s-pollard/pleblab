# motosu

Photo NAS, Immich host, and local backup target in the production homelab.

## hardware

- UGREEN NASync DXP2800
- Intel N100
- 8 GB memory
- 2.5 GbE onboard networking
- 2 × 6 TB Seagate ST6000NM0024 HDDs in a mirrored ZFS pool

## platform

TrueNAS SCALE

## role

Immich, photo/archive storage, snapshots, encrypted off-site backup, and local backup storage for other systems.
