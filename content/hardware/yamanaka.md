# yamanaka

Always-on general application server in the production homelab.

## hardware

- Dell OptiPlex 3070 Small Form Factor
- Intel Core i5-9500
- 16 GB DDR4-2666
- Patriot P300 512 GB NVMe
- onboard 1 GbE
- Intel X520-DA2 dual-port 10 GbE SFP+ adapter planned

## platform

Debian GNU/Linux 13 (trixie)

Docker Engine + Docker Compose

## role

General non-media applications, monitoring and logging, Uptime Kuma, AdGuard Home, SearXNG, and other retained services.
